[project]ion - audio credits
============================

Music (Audio/Music/*.ogg)
  Original. Rendered offline by tools/audio/ionmusic.py (repo Vasiniks/project.ion).
  No third-party samples. Same licence as the project.

Sound effects (Audio/Sfx/*.ogg)
  Original. Rendered offline by tools/audio/ionsfx.py. Everything is synthesised,
  except these layers, taken from Kenney's pack below:
    switch_press      <- impactMetal_light_000
    mover_stop        <- impactWood_heavy_000
    hatch_rise        <- impactWood_heavy_000
    collapse_crack    <- impactPlank_medium_000, impactPlank_medium_001

Footsteps (Audio/Sfx/Footsteps/step_*.ogg)
  Kenney "Impact Sounds" 1.0, by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released 2019-12-19.
  Source:   https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  Licence:  Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
            http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
            Attribution is not required. We credit Kenney anyway.
  Files:    footstep_concrete_000..004 -> step_tile_0..4 (high-passed) and step_stone_0..4
            footstep_wood_000..004     -> step_wood_0..4
            footstep_grass_000..004    -> step_grass_0..4
  Changes:  converted to mono 44.1 kHz, trimmed, filtered and normalised by tools/audio/ionsfx.py.
  The unmodified source files and Kenney's License.txt are kept in
  tools/audio/sources/kenney_impact_sounds/.
